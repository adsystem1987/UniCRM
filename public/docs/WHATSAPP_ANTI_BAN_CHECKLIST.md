# 🛡️ DFY UniCRM — WhatsApp Broadcast & Anti-Ban Best Practices

To protect your WhatsApp phone number, achieve 98%+ delivery rates, and build high conversion with prospective leads, follow this official operational checklist.

---

## 1. Number Preparation & Warm-Up (Crucial)

- [ ] **Use a Dedicated Outreach Number**: Keep your primary personal/support WhatsApp separate from cold outreach campaigns. (Ask UniCRM support if you need a dedicated virtual outbound line).
- [ ] **Complete Profile Verification**: Set your Business Name, Company Logo, Business Hours, and Website URL in WhatsApp settings.
- [ ] **Gradual Ramp-up / Warm-up (For new numbers < 30 days old)**:
  - **Days 1–3**: 30–50 messages/day (send to familiar contacts first to establish natural 2-way conversation history).
  - **Days 4–7**: 75–120 messages/day.
  - **Week 2+**: 250–500+ messages/day using UniCRM **Safe Mode**.

---

## 2. Campaign Setup & Pacing

- [ ] **Always Select Safe Mode or Ultra-Safe Mode**:
  - In UniCRM **Broadcasts -> Step 4 (Schedule & Send)**, select:
    - **Safe Mode**: 4–8 second human random jitter (Recommended).
    - **Ultra-Safe Mode**: 12–25 second delay for fresh numbers or cold audiences.
- [ ] **Personalize Every Message**:
  - Use `{{first_name}}` and `{{company}}` merge tags so that every outgoing message has a unique payload to bypass Meta's hash pattern filters.
- [ ] **Include Mandatory Opt-Out Instructions**:
  - Always close cold messages with:
    > *"Reply STOP to unsubscribe."*
  - **Why?** When a user replies "STOP", UniCRM automatically tags them into DND and excludes them from future blasts. This stops them from clicking WhatsApp's red **"Report as Spam"** button (which triggers account blocks).

---

## 3. Automated DND & Audience Hygiene

- [ ] **Automatic DND Tagging is Active**:
  - When contacts reply `STOP`, `UNSUBSCRIBE`, or `DNC`, UniCRM instantly tags them as `DNC / Opted-Out`.
  - Future broadcast audiences automatically exclude any contacts with this tag.
- [ ] **Target Verified Mobile Numbers Only**:
  - When using the UniCRM **Leads Finder (₹1/Lead)**, numbers are pre-filtered and standardized to `+91` mobile format. Avoid sending blasts to raw unverified landline lists.
- [ ] **Active Two-Way Engagement**:
  - Respond promptly to warm replies or enable UniCRM's **AI Auto-Reply** to answer common customer inquiries instantly.
